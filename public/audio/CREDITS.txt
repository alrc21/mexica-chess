Sound credits — Mexica Chess
============================

Every sound here is a real recording released under CC0 1.0 (public domain
dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Nothing is
synthesized. Credit is not required by the licence; it is given anyway.

Sources were the freesound.org HQ previews (128 kbps mp3) and one Kenney pack.

Processing common to all one-shots: cut from the source, mixed to mono,
resampled to 44.1 kHz, leading silence trimmed (-45 dB threshold, 3 ms kept),
4 ms fade-in (30–50 ms where the cut starts mid-sound), short fade-out,
peak-normalised to about -1.5 dBFS, encoded to mp3 112 kbps CBR (libmp3lame).
Loops: stereo, 44.1 kHz, made seamless by crossfading the segment's tail into
its head (equal-power, 1.5–2 s), one constant gain to about -23 LUFS
(true peak kept under -1 dBFS), mp3 128 kbps CBR.
